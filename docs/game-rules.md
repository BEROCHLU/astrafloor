# DEADSHIFT — Game Rules

[日本語](game-rules.jp.md) | English

A single-player zombie survival FPS for PC keyboard and mouse.

## Goal

Clear each wave by eliminating all enemies. Resupply at the shop between waves, then defeat the final boss in Wave 7 to win. You lose when HP reaches zero.

Normal starts at Wave 1. Hard has stronger, faster enemies and starts from Wave 2 (beginning in the supply shop before Wave 2 with ₡2,000 to prepare your loadout). There is no save feature: reloading the page, retrying, or returning to the title resets your run.

## Controls
 
Controls are kept minimal and standard so FPS beginners can easily adapt to keyboard and mouse play. Surviving and clearing the game requires utilizing strafe-shooting and slicing through zombie swarms on the move with melee attacks (V key).
 
| Action | Input |
|---|---|
| Move / look | W A S D / mouse |
| Fire / aim | Left click / hold right click |
| Sprint / jump | Left Shift / Space |
| Reload | R |
| Handgun / assault rifle / sniper rifle / RPG-7 | 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 (owned weapons only) |
| Melee attack | V |
| Throw grenade | G |
| Use Medical Kit | Q |
| Pause | Esc |
| Continue after clearing a wave | Space / Enter |

Choose **RESUME COMBAT** to resume. Sprinting consumes stamina and is unavailable while aiming.

## Staying Alive

- HP and armor both have a maximum of 100. Armor absorbs damage first.
- Clearing a wave fully restores HP. Armor, ammunition, and consumables must be replenished at the shop.
- Medical Kits restore 50 HP with a 10-second cooldown. You start with three and can carry up to three.
- Grenades and rockets can damage you with their explosions. Keep your distance and use cover.
- Aim for heads, sprint to evade enemy charges, and keep moving. Control recoil with the mouse; your aim does not automatically return after firing.

## Shop

Earn credits (₡) from kills and wave clears. Spend them on weapons, damage upgrades, ammunition, armor, Medical Kits, and grenades. The shop has no time limit. Select **NEXT WAVE** when ready.

- The G18C replaces your starting handgun and still uses key 1.
- The Katana permanently upgrades your V-key attack for the current run.
- The Ammo Pouch increases ammunition capacity and grenade capacity, and refills both on purchase.
- Equipment, upgrades, credits, and remaining supplies carry over between waves.
